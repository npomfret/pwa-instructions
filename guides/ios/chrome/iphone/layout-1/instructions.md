# Add to Home Screen from Chrome

Use Chrome on your iPhone. These steps install directly from its share sheet.

1. **Tap *Share*.** In Chrome, tap Share at the right end of the address bar at the top of the screen. Its icon is a square with an upward arrow.
2. **Tap *View More*.** If the share sheet is collapsed, tap View More, shown with a downward chevron, to reveal the remaining actions. If the action list is already visible, continue to step 3.
3. **Tap *Add to Home Screen*.** In the expanded action list, find and tap Add to Home Screen. Its icon is a rounded square containing a plus sign.
